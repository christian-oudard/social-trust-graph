# v0.3 → v0.4: review round 3 and dispositions

Three reviews of v0.3 are in: red team (`review3_X_redteam.md`), practice
(`review3_P_practice.md`) and legal accuracy (`review3_L_legal.md`). A formal review is
pending. Every exploit was reproduced before it was acted on; each is now a regression in
`x7`, or an edit to `s1`–`s6` and `x2`–`x6`.

## Replaced: answerability = coverage → answerability = operation

v0.3 made P answer for everything its grants covered. The red team broke it four ways:

| Finding | Exploit on v0.3 |
|---|---|
| Z1 | A narrow grant to a vendor-run agent made Alice answer for the vendor's other customers |
| Z2 | Feeding Alice's output into an unowned instance made her answer for it (griefing) |
| Z3 | Pins, revocation and `until` switched answerability off: scope immunity returned |
| Z4 | A copy made by someone else was answered for by the original's principal (framing) |

v0.4 splits the two questions. **Authority** is still coverage within scope. **Answerability**
follows **operation**: `op(I,P)`, recorded by `operator(I,P)`, with defaults (a person runs
their body, a grantor runs a parentless root it created, operation passes down continue
edges, a copy is run by whoever made it). P answers for what P runs, for the acts P
authorized (`acts_for/3`), and for what P steered. Feeds carry knowledge only. Imputation
follows operation, not coverage. Succession (`continuation_of`) becomes a takeover of
operation, which also fixes Z9 (it previously needed a recognized grant and almost never
fired).

## Wording (legal review)

* **No warranty of authority** is now stated as a stipulation (deemed notice as an agreed
  attribution procedure, cf. UNCITRAL MLAC Art 7(1)), with the positive law that refuses
  it (CA 2006 s.40, Directive 2017/1132 Art 9(2), RUPA §303(f)). A1.
* Vitiation "void" is labelled a simplification of voidability (R2d §§164, 175). A2.
  Dual-agent releases likewise. B17.
* Securities cite R3d Agency §3.12–3.13; "collateral" is gone. B12.
* Parfit: "compare", with the wife as the third party. B1.
* s2: R2d §318(1), not (2); GDPR Art 28(2) as the controller's *specific* authorisation. B2, B3.
* x2: continuity of enterprise (*Turner*), inheriting good standing only. B4.
* Capacities after Kurki's bundle theory, not "Hohfeldian"; `actor` includes the
  competence to bind oneself; Leibo supports the unbundling as a whole. B5, B7, B8.
* Hohfeld's power/liability covers authority only; answerability follows the
  instrumentality view (R3d §1.04 cmt e, MLAC Art 7). B6.
* UCAN → ZCAP-LD `parentCapability`. B9. Defaults no longer claimed to replace DDL. B10.
  Hart's rule of recognition. B11. ADICO / IG 2.0 mapping made precise. B13. Debtor slot
  for powers (Symboleo). B14. "Drawn on", "No new logic was invented", "The gap
  addressed". B15.
* References and evals are "modelled as" warranties. D.
* README lists the deliberate simplifications (void vs voidable, dual agency, §5.03
  imputation of all knowledge, non-assignable privileges vs R2d §317).
