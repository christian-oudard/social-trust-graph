# v0.1 → v0.2: review findings and dispositions

Four adversarial reviews of v0.1 (full texts in `notes/review_R*.md`). Each finding was
reproduced against v0.1 with clingo before being acted on. Dispositions prefer changes
that remove or merge primitives.

## Unifying change: every registry change is an act

Three reviewers found the same root gap independently. These base facts had no author,
so nobody checked their scope and there was no time of effect:

* lineage edges;
* releases and revocations;
* role appointments;
* evidence.

A debtor could revoke its own NDA, and a recorded copy edge conferred authority.

In v0.2, every change to normative state is an act of some invocation, attributed
through `acts_for` like any other act. It takes effect only in scope and only at the
act's time.

| Registry change | Act | Effective iff |
|---|---|---|
| create commitment | `created(C,I)` | as before |
| release | `releases(I,C)` | I acts for the creditor, in scope `release` |
| revoke | `revokes(I,G)` | I acts for the grantor, in scope `revoke` |
| copy / merge | `edge(I1,I2,copy\|merge)` is I1's act `copying`/`merging` | authority follows only if the grant follows that kind; **responsibility** follows whenever the copying act was in someone's scope |
| content change | swap / rewrite on a continue edge is I1's act `swapping`/`rewriting` | so "do not swap without notice" is an ordinary avoid commitment |
| attestation | a **warranty** (commitment of mode `warrant`) by the attester | the attester answers for it |

## Findings and dispositions

| # | Finding (reviewer) | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | All person bodies shared hash `opaque`: humans "forgot" at once, and one person's reference assured everyone (R1) | Opaque hashes are `opaque(Id)`, unique per bearer. Scrubbing and rollback need fully observable content |
| 2 | Scrubbing by global content equality: cross-lineage erasure, forgetting after a step with no change (R1, R4, R3) | Scrub only on reverting to the content of an *ancestor* upstream of every learning of X. Learning without a content change is a type error |
| 3 | Pin laundering: sub-grants escape parent pins and followed edges (R1, R3; check C6 finds it) | Caveats accumulate. Effective pins are the union down the chain; followed kinds are the intersection. A principal's own `all` root is exempt |
| 4 | Consent as power makes the consenter answer for, and know what, the processor knows. A dual agent excuses everything (R1, R3) | New mode **permit** with the same scope fields as a grant. Its only effect is `excused`. Powers no longer excuse |
| 5 | Fork-and-disown: a copy the grant does not follow escapes all responsibility, refuting L2 as stated (R3) | Copying is an act. Whoever's scope covered the copying answers for the copy's conduct and warrants its authority. Authority still needs `follows copy`. L2 is restated: both are scoped, and copying is in scope |
| 6 | Releases, revocations and founding are unauthored. Revocation can be backdated (R1, R3) | Release and revoke are acts, effective from the act's time. `founded` is renamed **recognized**: the adjudicator's rule of recognition, made explicit |
| 7 | Party-vs-delegate prejudged: under "delegate", machines still owe, claim and grant (R1) | Standing becomes a Hohfeldian **capacity bundle**: owe, claim, empower, answer. The three hypotheses are `delegate` {}, `actor` {owe, answer} ("duties without rights") and `party` {all four} |
| 8 | Slots were sets: an added LoRA hash kept a pin; a test on an absent slot supported everything (R4, R3) | One hash per slot, and slots come from a closed vocabulary. Layered weights hash together. Tests or pins on an absent slot are type errors |
| 9 | Envelope product: per-slot sets allow untested combinations (R3) | A pin is a set of *configurations*, `pin(C,K,Slot,H)`: conjunctive within K, disjunctive across K |
| 10 | Evidence was an unaccountable sort; self-issued `clean` launders leaks (R1, R3) | **Evidence is a warranty**: mode `warrant`, debtor the attester, range fixed by pins. Tests, sanitization claims and references are warranties, and a false one is the attester's defection. Human references must expire. The evidence sort is removed |
| 11 | Substrate is eliminable (R1) | Substrate is a named content map (input sugar): `runs(I,S), part(S,…)` becomes `slot(I,Slot,H)`. The substrate sort is removed |
| 12 | Acts were an unacknowledged sort (R1) | **act** is acknowledged as a sort: the domain vocabulary |
| 13 | Conditional grants covered before their trigger (R4) | Coverage needs detachment. A grant's trigger act is performed by the grantor's side. Human-in-the-loop approval is then just a conditional grant |
| 14 | Drift-invariant enforcement: a surety can drift too (R1) | **Collateral**: a conditional power *held by the creditor* over the debtor's performance. Repair needs no future cooperation by anyone but the creditor. L9 is restated |
| 15 | Vacancy: a breach with no defector (R3, R1) | `appointer(R,Q)`: the appointer answers for breaches during a vacancy, or when the holder cannot answer |
| 16 | Vitiation: duress, fraud and prompt injection were invisible (R1, R4) | `induced(I,Q)`: a promise induced by its own creditor is void, and a breach induced by the creditor is excused |
| 17 | Re-registration of a defaulting lineage (R3) | A principal recognized over a lineage *continuation* of a defector's invocation inherits its open defections. A fresh instantiation from the same weights (content without lineage) does not |
| 18 | Grain dependence (R4) | Documented grain: an invocation is one sampling request and response. Tool calls, including parallel ones, are its acts |
| 19 | Per-act caps are not budgets (R3, R4) | Kept as per-act limits. Budgets are expressible as exclusive tokens (existing machinery); aggregates are deferred |
| 20 | Standing without solvency (R3) | Documented: answerability is not collectibility. Collateral (14) is the collectible form |
| 21 | Disclosure `knows + with` is coarse (R4) | Kept as conservative non-interference. Knowledge now also flows along `feeds(I1,I2)` (messages, shared stores), not only lineage |
